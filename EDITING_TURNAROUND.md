# Per-video turnaround

Human requirement: the complete per-video run should take 3–4 hours, including preparation, generation/queue waits, recording, editing, rendering and actual audiovisual/4 FPS review. Initial app construction is separate from a normal video run. Record each stage and external waiting without excluding it from total elapsed time.

Reuse completed media, receipts, source review, camera paths and approved methods. Do not repeat discovery, scans, generation or whole-video rendering without a specific unresolved defect. Repair affected sections, then assemble verified sections. Source matching, natural speech, every required style, real UI recordings, paid-media limits and artifact QA remain required.

The first 751-second candidate missed the target: it included a roughly seven-hour approval stall and about 155 minutes of asset preparation, followed by rendering, repairs and QA. That candidate was subsequently rejected for missing/clipped speech and style defects. Delays and repair rounds count toward elapsed time; the early export was not completion.

The owner-approved result is the October 18 pause-trimmed master: 27,400 frames / 913.333333 seconds at 30fps, retaining all 177 cues at native 1x. The owner accepted the longer runtime to preserve natural speech. Review revisions, final trimming and private publication followed the early candidate; the 3–4 hour target has not been demonstrated.

The current app avoids repeated work through durable jobs, stable request keys, section dependency hashes, typed effects and conditional feedback checks. Record render frames per second, stage durations, provider waits and repair rounds for the next full run. Judge the target from that end-to-end result, not a fast render.
